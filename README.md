## Focus Areas

- **Quantitative Finance & Risk:** Limit Order Book (LOB) microstructure, execution friction & alpha decay, multivariate time-series econometrics (VAR/IRF, Engle-Granger, VECM), EWMA covariance dynamics, and Random Matrix Theory (Marchenko-Pastur) spectral filtering.
- **Financial Machine Learning & Reinforcement Learning:** Automated SEC EDGAR XBRL US-GAAP pipelines, corporate distress modeling (Altman Z-Score & Ohlson O-Score), Random Forest risk classification, Isolation Forest anomaly detection, and Bellman/MDP dynamic allocation policies.
- **Algorithmic Trading Engineering:** End-to-end daily market ingestion and paper-trading workflows via Alpaca API, combining technical feature engineering, scikit-learn classifiers, PyTorch LSTM networks, and vectorized backtesting.
- **Business Intelligence & Web3:** Interactive executive analytics apps with Streamlit, Plotly, and PostgreSQL/Supabase, alongside decentralized pari-mutuel smart contracts in Solidity and Hardhat.
